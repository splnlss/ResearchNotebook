# Research notebook

Short, linked pages for research, options, recommendations, solutions,
story structure, transcripts, and ideas. Each content page has a one-line
synopsis in its `description` field.

## Browse

- [Notes](notes/index.md) - checked research and synthesized findings.
- [Ideas](ideas/index.md) - creative exploration and open possibilities.
- [Drafts](drafts/index.md) - human writing and work in progress.
- [Raw](raw/index.md) - unchanged source captures and transcripts.
- [Log](log.md) - material changes, newest first.
